Gembel Klip Uploader

Gembel Klip Uploader is a personal automation tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Gembel Klip to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube through the official YouTube Data API.

What it does

  • Uploads video files produced by the channel owner to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Sets basic metadata for those uploads (title, description, category, privacy status).
  • Runs only on the owner's own computer, under the owner's own Google account.

What it does not do

  • It is not a public service and has no other users.
  • It does not access, collect, or share any other person's data.
  • It does not read analytics, comments, or subscriber information.
